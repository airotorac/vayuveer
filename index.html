Autonomy stack

One brain,
seven airframes

Every Vayuveer aircraft flies the same flight controller, the same ground station and the same permission workflow. Train a crew once and they can operate the whole fleet.

Flight control

Vayu FCS

Triple-redundant flight computer with dissimilar IMUs. Loses a motor, a GNSS fix or a link and keeps flying the mission.

Permissions

NPNT native

No permission, no take-off. Permission artefacts from Digital Sky are fetched, signed and logged on the aircraft before every flight.

Command link

BVLOS ready

Dual-path link over LTE and a 2.4/5.8 GHz mesh, with AES-256 encryption and automatic fail-over between the two.

Environment

Monsoon hardened

Sealed motors and conformal-coated boards. Rated for driving rain, red dust and 45 °C flight-line temperatures.

Payloads

60-second swap

One mechanical and electrical payload bus across the fleet. Gimbal, LiDAR, winch, tank or cargo bay, no tools required.

Ground control

One console

Plan, fly and review any aircraft from the same rugged tablet. Flight logs sync to your servers, not ours.

Compliance

Built to the
Drone Rules, 2022

Indian airspace is zoned, certified and logged. Vayuveer aircraft are engineered for that system rather than adapted to it afterwards.

7Type-certification

Every airframe on the DGCA type-certification pathway through a QCI-approved lab.

UINDigital Sky

Unique Identification Number issued per aircraft; flight logs retained as the rules require.

RPCPilot training

Remote Pilot Certificate courses through a DGCA-approved RPTO partner.

4classesMicro to Large

Fleet spans Micro, Small, Medium and Large classes under the 2022 rules.

100%Made in India

Airframe, flight controller and ground station designed and assembled in India.
