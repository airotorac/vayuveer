    The stack

    One brain,
    four airframes

    The AX4, X8, X9 and FX7 share the same open flight stack, the same handheld ground station and the same permission workflow. Train a crew once and they can fly the whole line.

    Positioning

    RTK to the centimetre

    Dual-antenna RTK on the AX4, carrier-phase RTK on the X8 and X9. Heading from the antennas, not the compass, so steel structures don't pull the aircraft off line.

    Command link

    Digital link, 5G failover

    Long-range digital RC and video out to 15 km on the heavy-lift platforms. If the link drops, the AX4 fails over to 5G cellular automatically and the mission carries on.

    Payloads

    Quick-release everything

    The AX4's gimbal and battery swap without tools. The X8 and X9 take firefighting, rescue and logistics modules on one mount. The FX7 drops a 1.5 kg payload on command.

    Environment

    Built for 36 °C

    Fan-ducted battery and avionics bays, sealed motors and conformal-coated boards. Full endurance is rated at 36 °C ambient, not at a lab temperature.

    Tethered

    Unlimited time on station

    The X8 and X9 fly tethered on ground power and water for facade cleaning and fire suppression, so endurance is set by the shift, not the battery.

    Training

    Sim first, then fly

    Crews build stick time and release discipline in the FPV simulator, then transfer straight onto the FX7. The same ground station flies every aircraft in the line.

    What does Vayuveer build?

    Four aircraft: the AX4 folding surveillance and mapping quadcopter, the X8 and X9 heavy-lift octa-quads for firefighting, water rescue and logistics, and the FX7 7-inch FPV payload quadcopter.

    What is the AX4's endurance?

    About 34 minutes of hover at 36 °C with a 20 percent reserve, or about 38 minutes in cruise, on a 6S 16 Ah solid-state battery. These are design estimates from calculation, not flight-test results.

    Are Vayuveer drones made in India?

    Yes. The airframes, flight stack integration and ground stations are designed and assembled in India.

    Do the aircraft comply with the Drone Rules?

    They are engineered for the Indian system from the start: Digital Sky registration, NPNT, and the DGCA type-certification pathway. The AX4 and FX7 fall in the Small class, the X8 and X9 at the Small to Medium boundary.

    How much can the X8 and X9 carry?

    The X8 carries about 20 kg and the X9 about 30 kg, with firefighting, rescue and logistics modules. Both can fly tethered for unlimited time on station.
