Screening interview

Thank you for applying to Vayuveer. This is a short voice interview with our AI interviewer about your experience with UAVs, sensors, aerodynamics and software. Our team reviews every interview before deciding.

10 to 15 minDuration
Voice callFormat
EN / HI / GULanguages

Before you start

  • Sit in a quiet place and use earphones with a microphone if you can.
  • Use Chrome, Edge or Safari on a laptop or phone with a stable internet connection.
  • Allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  • Take the interview once, on your own, without notes or help.

How it works

  1. Fill in your name, email and mobile number in the form below. Each candidate can take the interview only once.
  2. Press the Start interview button that appears at the bottom right of this page.
  3. Tell the interviewer your name, college, branch and year.
  4. Answer about ten questions. You may answer in English, Hindi or Gujarati.
  5. If you have no experience in an area, say so honestly. That is normal for an intern.
  6. The interviewer ends the call when it is finished. You can then close this page.

Your details

Enter your details to unlock the interview. You get one attempt, so start only when you are ready.

Ready?

Press Start interview at the bottom right corner of the screen. Once the call starts, your one attempt is used, so do not close or reload this page until the interviewer ends the call.

Interview already taken

Our records show this interview has already been taken. Each candidate gets one attempt. If your call failed because of a technical problem, please contact the Vayuveer team.

The interview is recorded and transcribed so the Vayuveer team can review it. Your name, email, mobile number and the recording are stored and used only for internship selection. Our team will contact you with the result.
