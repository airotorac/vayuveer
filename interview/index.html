Internship programme

Screening interview

Thank you for applying to Vayuveer. This is a short voice interview with our AI interviewer about your experience with UAVs, sensors, aerodynamics and software. Our team reviews every interview before deciding.

10 to 15 minDuration
Voice callFormat
EN / HI / GULanguages

Before you start

  • Sit in a quiet place and use earphones with a microphone if you can.
  • Use Chrome, Edge or Safari on a laptop or phone with a stable internet connection.
  • Allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  • Take the interview once, on your own, without notes or help.

How it works

  1. Press the Start interview button at the bottom right of this page.
  2. Tell the interviewer your name, college, branch and year. Then give a phone number or email.
  3. Answer about ten questions. You may answer in English, Hindi or Gujarati.
  4. If you have no experience in an area, say so honestly. That is normal for an intern.
  5. The interviewer ends the call when it is finished. You can then close this page.

Ready?

Press Start interview at the bottom right corner of the screen.

Button not showing? Open the interview in a new tab

The interview is recorded and transcribed so the Vayuveer team can review it. The recording is used only for internship selection. Our team will contact you with the result.
